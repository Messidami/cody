OUR WORK

Driving lasting impact by investing in community-driven change

Disaster Relief

We stand with families during times of crisis, providing emergency aid and rebuilding support after natural disasters, especially in rural areas often left behind.

Music & Mental Health

Inspired by Cody Johnson’s journey, we support music therapy programs and mental health services that use the power of song to heal and unite.

Veteran Support

We are dedicated to supporting veterans with career programs, mental health resources, and family assistance to honor their service and sacrifice.

Youth Leadership

From mentorship to scholarships, we empower the next generation of leaders to rise up and give back to their communities.

Future Generations

Our work today creates a brighter tomorrow — for families, for youth, and for all communities striving to grow stronger together.

Community Concerts

We host benefit concerts and outreach events to raise awareness and funds for our programs, creating moments that bring people together through music and compassion.

Family & Emergency Support

The foundation provides urgent financial assistance, temporary housing, and emotional care for families navigating hardship due to illness or disaster.
