Cody Supports Public Health

Cody Johnson is more than music — he's a voice for real change. As a strong supporter of the U.S. Department of Health & Human Services (HHS), Cody is helping fans and communities access real-world opportunities like grants, healthcare resources, and wellness programs.

  • ✔️ Family healthcare grants
  • ✔️ Child nutrition support
  • ✔️ Mental health resources
  • ✔️ Emergency financial assistance
  • ✔️ COVID-19 recovery support
  • ✔️ Housing and utility relief programs

Did You Know?

In 2024, over 2 million families benefited from HHS programs — and Codyy is helping raise awareness to reach even more people this year.

“Every voice matters. Every family matters. Let’s create a healthier, more hopeful future — together.”
- Cody Johnson

How It Works

Codyy provides curated access to verified health-related assistance from HHS. While Codyy does not process grants directly, we guide you to the right resources where you can apply officially.

  1. Step 1: Visit the HHS site
  2. Step 2: Browse available programs and resources
  3. Step 3: Check eligibility requirements and documentation
  4. Step 4: Submit your application through the official portal
  5. Step 5: Track your application and receive updates
